Privacy Policy

Effective 10 September 2026 · Last updated 10 September 2026

Legal · Terms of Service

The short version

Your medication information stays on your phone. We do not have a server that stores it. We cannot see your medications, your doses, your photos, or whether you took them.

The app sends us one thing only: an automatic report when the app crashes, so we can fix it. Those reports do not contain your medication information.

There are no accounts, no passwords, and no sign-in. We do not sell anything and we do not show advertisements.

1 · Who we are

ADHERE is a free application developed by ADHERE, a student organisation at the University of Pennsylvania, with faculty guidance. The app is published on the App Store by an individual student developer. Contact: klarika0708@gmail.com.

ADHERE is not a healthcare provider, a pharmacy, or a covered entity under HIPAA. We are not affiliated with, and do not act on behalf of, any clinic, insurer, or pharmaceutical company.

2 · What the app stores on your device

All of the following is written only to storage on your own phone. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhy
Medication names, doses, notesTo show you what to take and when
Reminder timesTo schedule notifications
Photos of your medication that you choose to takeTo help you recognise the right medication
Records of doses taken or skippedTo show your streak and adherence over time
Your answer to the onboarding questionTo show you relevant content
A display name, if you enter oneTo personalise the app

Because this data lives only on your device, it is removed when you delete the app. It is not backed up to us. If you lose your phone or delete the app, this information cannot be recovered by us or by you.

3 · What leaves your device

Crash and error reports

We use Sentry, a third-party error monitoring service, to receive automatic reports when the app crashes or hits an error. A report may include: the type of error and where in our code it happened, your device model, your operating system version, the app version, and the approximate time.

Crash reports do not include your medication names, doses, dose history, photos, or display name. We configure Sentry not to collect personally identifying information.

Sentry processes this data on our behalf. See sentry.io/privacy.

Links you choose to open

The Learn section links to health information published by others, such as MedlinePlus, the CDC, and the NIH. If you tap a link, you leave ADHERE and that website's own privacy policy applies. We do not control those sites and we do not receive information about what you read there.

4 · What we never collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, and we do not show advertisements
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not sell or share personal information with data brokers
  • We do not use your information to train machine learning models

6 · Health information

Medication names can reveal sensitive facts about your health. We treat that seriously, and our answer is structural rather than a promise: we designed the app so that this information never reaches us in the first place. There is no account to breach and no database of user medications to leak.

Anyone with access to your unlocked phone can see your medications in the app. Protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock is the most effective privacy step you can take.

7 · Children

ADHERE is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. A parent or guardian managing a child's medication should use the app on their own device.

8 · Your choices

  • Delete everything: deleting the app removes all of the data it stored on your device. There is nothing held on our side to request or erase.
  • Turn off reminders: in your phone's notification settings, at any time.
  • Refuse crash reporting: contact us and we will explain how to opt out in the current version.

Because we hold no personal information about you, requests to access, correct, port, or delete your data have nothing on our side to act on. Everything is already in your hands.

9 · Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information — for example, if a future version adds accounts or syncing — we will update this policy and change the date at the top. Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
