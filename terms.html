Legal · Privacy Policy

ADHERE is not medical advice. It is a reminder and education tool. It does not know your medical history, it cannot check whether your medications interact, and it cannot tell you whether a dose is right for you.

Always follow the instructions from your doctor, pharmacist, or the label on your medication. If those instructions differ from anything in this app, follow your doctor or pharmacist, not the app. If you think you are having a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

The short version

ADHERE is a free app that reminds you to take medication you have entered yourself. It is built by students, provided as-is, and it can fail. Reminders can be delayed or missed for reasons outside our control, such as your phone's battery settings.

Do not rely on this app alone for medication that matters.

2 · What ADHERE does and does not do

It does: store medication information you type in, send reminders at times you choose, record what you tell it you took or skipped, and link to health information published by reputable third parties.

It does not: provide medical advice, diagnose anything, check for drug interactions, verify that what you entered is correct, communicate with your doctor or pharmacy, or monitor your health.

Everything the app shows you is based on information you entered yourself. If you enter it incorrectly, the app will faithfully remind you of the wrong thing.

3 · Reminders are not guaranteed

This deserves stating plainly. Notification delivery depends on your phone's operating system, your battery and power-saving settings, whether the app has been closed or restarted, network conditions, and Do Not Disturb or Focus modes. Some of these are outside our control and outside your control.

We have tested reminder reliability and we work to make it as dependable as we can, but we cannot promise a reminder will always arrive on time, or at all. For medication where a missed dose is dangerous, use an additional method as well. An alarm, a pill organiser, or another person will all serve.

4 · Your responsibilities

  • Do not use the app to manage another person's medication without their knowledge and consent
